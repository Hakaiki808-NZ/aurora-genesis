## Short LinkedIn / general social

We're building Kyro around two practical ideas: teams should be able to work together with AI, and useful knowledge should be modular.

Our public research repository includes Genesis starter knowledge packs for Spanish, te reo Māori and physics. Kyro's private-alpha collaboration work includes role-scoped projects and discussion, with a public sample-data preview.

## Developer community post

Title: Building a modular AI research stack with separate interface, model and engine boundaries

We're working on Kyro (interface), Genesis (model/reference knowledge packs), and Aurora (restricted execution authority). Our open-source repository has a reference knowledge-pack lifecycle, API/contract documents and test harnesses. The private-alpha collaboration design uses owner/editor/viewer roles and project discussions.

Not claims: production secure Aurora cloud execution, AGI, hosted specialist marketplace, or validated trained-foundation-model capability. We're looking for substantive criticism of the trust boundary and clear reproducible issues.

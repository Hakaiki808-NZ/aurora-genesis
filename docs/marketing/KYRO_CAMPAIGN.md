**Campaign status:** Approved technical narrative and draft assets, **not** a public-beta launch or purchased ad campaign. These messages are for the review and prelaunch/early-access outreach phase. Keep capacity and release gates as in docs/releases/PUBLIC_BETA_GATE_2026_11_07.md.

## Brand line

**Build together. Grow with knowledge.**

Supporting line: **A shared workspace for your projects and an AI model designed to expand with new knowledge.**

## What is real now

- **Kyro collaboration, private alpha:** owner/editor/viewer project roles; expiring one-time invite tokens; team messages; server-versioned project files. Invite-only. Not real-time Google Docs-like co-editing.
- **Genesis free knowledge packs, development reference:** install, search, disable, replace and remove data-only subject packs in the reference model runtime, including Spanish Basics, Te Reo Māori Basics and Physics Foundations. Not automatically deployed to user cloud accounts.
- **Open development:** users can review the repository and test the read-only V3 workspace preview.
- **Aurora boundary:** protected Engine execution is **not attached** to the public Kyro experience.

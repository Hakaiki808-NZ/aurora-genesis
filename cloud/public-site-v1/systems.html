THE ARCHITECTURE

Responsibility stays where it belongs.

Each layer has a defined purpose and cannot silently inherit the authority of another.

01 / ENGINE

Aurora

Admits and supervises workloads, manages lifecycle, resources, operational integrity and independent execution controls.

Governs execution

Governs cognition

INTERNAL GOVERNANCE

Boundaries, not blind trust.

Model versus Engine authority

Genesis's Alpha and Omega Guardians oversee semantic governance; Aurora's Supreme Guardian oversees operational integrity. Aurora-owned Angels are independently controlled inspection and interlock entities, not Model-controlled assistants.

Transparent where safe

Public contracts, tests, reference modules and research results can be reviewed. Engine-private code, signing material, sensitive protection mechanisms and credentials are not published.
